Test yourself

Circle one answer for each question, then check your answers in the simulation.

1. In which order do you loosen the force frame screws?
  1. 1 → 2 → 3
  2. 3 → 2 → 1
  3. Any order
  4. 2 → 1 → 3
2. When do you remove the PnP cover cap from the socket?
  1. Before opening the force frame
  2. After the carrier frame and CPU are in the rail frame
  3. After the cooler is fitted
  4. You never remove it
3. On an sTR5 system, where are the fragile contacts (pins)?
  1. On the underside of the CPU
  2. In the motherboard socket
  3. On the carrier frame
  4. On the force frame
4. How do you tighten the force frame screws?
  1. In the order 1 → 2 → 3 until the torque driver clicks (about 1.6 N·m)
  2. As tight as possible with a normal screwdriver
  3. 3 → 2 → 1, by hand only
  4. Any order, to about 5 N·m
5. Your new cooler has thermal paste already applied. What do you do?
  1. Add a large blob on top for extra cooling
  2. Fit it as supplied without adding more paste
  3. Wipe it off and use one pea-sized dot
  4. Put paste on the motherboard instead
