Open the link and work through the 3D disc brake simulation (20–30 minutes). Use headphones and press Sound on.

Tasks

  1. Guided tour: follow all 9 steps.
  2. Hold to brake: watch the fluid, the piston and the pads. Try slow motion.
  3. Inspect: tap each part and note what it does and what to check.
  4. Spot the fault: add worn pads, then air in the fluid. Describe the pedal feel and stopping distance for each.
  5. Test yourself: answer the 5 questions.
