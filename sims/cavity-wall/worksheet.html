Tasks

  1. Guided tour: watch all 9 stages.
  2. Build it: assemble the wall in the right order and place the wall ties.
  3. Spot the faults: find all 6 faults. For each one, write down what is wrong and what it causes.
  4. Test yourself: answer the 5 questions. Use "Show me" if you get stuck.

Test yourself

3. How high above the outside ground should the DPC be?

  2. At least 75 mm

4. Where do partial-fill insulation boards go?
  1. Against the outer leaf
  2. In the middle of the cavity
  3. Against the inner leaf, keeping at least 50 mm clear cavity

5. What is the job of a weep hole above a cavity tray?

  2. To let you check the wall ties
