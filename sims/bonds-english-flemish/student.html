Help

Moving around

  • Drag to turn the wall all the way round.
  • Pinch or scroll to zoom. Two-finger drag or right-drag to move sideways.
  • Tap or hover over a brick to see its name and job.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Space play or pause
  • ← → previous or next stage
  • 1–4 camera views
  • L labels, J straight joints
  • + − add or remove a course
  • Esc close a panel

Sound

Narration reads each stage out loud. Captions show the same words. Use the speaker button to turn sound off.

Colours and patterns are for learning only. Real bricks in a wall are usually all the same colour.
