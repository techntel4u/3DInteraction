Open the link and practise the information part of your speaking exam (10–15 minutes). Use headphones and keep the sound on.

Tasks

  1. Read the task and choose where you will write: on the screen or on paper.
  2. Listen to Jenny. She says each piece of information once.
  3. Ask Jenny to repeat, say it slowly or spell it. Use full questions, for example: Can you spell that, please?
  4. Write down all three pieces of information.
  5. Finish with all three exam checklist items ticked.
  6. Print or screenshot your results and check them with a partner. Then try a new set.

Test yourself
