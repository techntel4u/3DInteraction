Tasks

  1. Guided tour: follow all 11 stages.
  2. Stages 2–8: say which valves are open and shut at each step.
  3. Stage 9: explain why the left ventricle wall is thicker than the right.
  4. Turn Labels off, then tap each part and name it before the tip appears.
  5. Stage 11: call out each stop before the blood cell reaches it.
  6. Test yourself: answer the 5 questions.

Test yourself

Circle one answer for each question, then check your answers in the simulation.

1. Blood returning from the body enters the heart first in which chamber?
  1. Left atrium
  2. Right atrium
  3. Right ventricle
  4. Left ventricle
2. Which valve stops blood flowing back into the right atrium when the right ventricle contracts?
  1. Bicuspid valve
  2. Aortic valve
  3. Tricuspid valve
  4. Pulmonary valve
3. Which blood vessel carries deoxygenated blood away from the heart?
  1. Pulmonary artery
  2. Pulmonary vein
  3. Aorta
  4. Vena cava
4. Why is the wall of the left ventricle thicker than the wall of the right ventricle?
  1. It holds more oxygen
  2. It pumps blood at higher pressure round the whole body
  3. It contracts before the right ventricle
  4. It has to push blood through the septum
5. Which order is correct for blood going from the body to the lungs and back?
  1. Vena cava → right atrium → right ventricle → pulmonary artery → lungs → pulmonary vein → left atrium → left ventricle → aorta
  2. Vena cava → left atrium → left ventricle → pulmonary vein → lungs → pulmonary artery → right atrium → right ventricle → aorta
  3. Aorta → right atrium → right ventricle → pulmonary vein → lungs → pulmonary artery → left atrium → left ventricle → vena cava
  4. Vena cava → right ventricle → right atrium → pulmonary artery → lungs → pulmonary vein → left ventricle → left atrium → aorta
