1. Which vessel has a thick, muscular, elastic wall and carries blood at high pressure?
  1. Vein
  2. Capillary
  3. Artery
  4. Venule
2. Why do veins have valves?
  1. To speed blood up
  2. To stop blood flowing backwards
  3. To add oxygen to the blood
  4. To make the pulse
3. Why are capillaries the place where gases are exchanged?
  1. Their wall is one cell thick
  2. They have valves
  3. They carry blood at the highest pressure
  4. They are the widest vessels
4. Which part of the blood carries most of the oxygen?
  1. Plasma
  2. White blood cells
  3. Platelets
  4. Red blood cells
5. During exercise, what happens to the arterioles supplying the working muscles?
  1. They vasoconstrict, so less blood flows
  2. They vasodilate, so more blood flows
  3. They close completely
  4. Nothing changes
