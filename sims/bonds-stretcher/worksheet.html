Tasks

  1. Guided tour: follow all 8 stages.
  2. Stage 2: explain what a straight joint is and why it weakens a wall.
  3. Stage 5: describe the half lap in your own words.
  4. Stage 6: say where the half bats go and why.
  5. Stage 7: work out the height of 6 courses at 75 mm gauge.
  6. Spot the fault: find the mistake and explain it.
  7. Test yourself: answer the 5 questions.
