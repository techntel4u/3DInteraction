    What you'll learn

    By the end, you can

    • say how long something has been true with I've had it for... / since...
    • choose for (a length of time) or since (the start)
    • use the past simple with a finished time: I lifted some boxes on Monday, I took some tablets last night
    • answer Have you ever...? with No, I've never... or No, I haven't
    • explain a health problem to a GP in clear, full sentences

    Common mistakes

    ✗ I have it since Monday.
    ✓ I've had it since Monday.
    ✗ I've had it since four days.
    ✓ I've had it for four days.
    ✗ I have lifted boxes on Monday.
    ✓ I lifted some boxes on Monday.
    ✗ I worked there for two years. (You still work there.)
    ✓ I've worked there for two years.
    ✗ I never had back pain before.
    ✓ I've never had back pain before.

    Curriculum link

    Adult ESOL Core Curriculum, Entry 3 (Speaking).

    Sc/E3.4a Express clearly statements of fact: use with some accuracy suitable verb forms to make clear the time to which the statement of fact refers, including the past simple and present perfect, together with appropriate time markers.

    Sc/E3.3b Ask questions to obtain personal or factual information, for example How long have you worked there?

    Entry 3 key grammar includes the present perfect with since/for, ever/never and yet/already. Check the wording against your awarding body's Entry 3 ESOL Skills for Life specification.
