At the doctor's: present perfect and past simple

Transcript of the narration · Entry 3
▶ Open the simulation

Everything the narrator says, in order. Use it if you can't use sound, or to revise afterwards.

The conversation

1. Dr Shah: Hello, come in and take a seat. What seems to be the problem today?

Your notes: Your back hurts. Tell the doctor what has happened. A good answer: I've hurt my back.

2. Dr Shah: How long have you had the pain?

Your notes: Today is Friday. The pain started on Monday. A good answer: I've had it since Monday. Or: I've had it for four days.

3. Dr Shah: I see. And what happened on Monday? How did you hurt it?

Your notes: On Monday you were at work. You lifted some heavy boxes. A good answer: I lifted some heavy boxes at work.

4. Dr Shah: Have you taken anything for the pain?

Your notes: Yes. Ibuprofen tablets. You took them last night. A good answer: Yes, I took some ibuprofen last night.

5. Dr Shah: Have you ever had back pain before?

Your notes: No. Never. This is the first time. A good answer: No, I've never had back pain before. Or: No, I haven't.

6. Dr Shah: You said you lift boxes at work. Do you still work there? How long have you worked there?

Your notes: Yes, you still work at the warehouse. You started two years ago, in 2024. A good answer: I've worked there for two years. Or: I've worked there since 2024.

Dr Shah's advice

At the end

Thank you, that's really helpful. Back pain like this usually gets better within a few weeks. Try to stay active and keep doing your normal things. Don't stay in bed for a long time. A hot water bottle wrapped in a towel can help. Always read the instructions on painkillers. If it isn't getting better, come back and see me.
